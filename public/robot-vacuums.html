Robot vacuum buying comparison — Turkey

Research snapshot: 18 November 2026. Prepared for Sinan.

Your home and selection criteria

Known: small home, mostly level, laminate flooring, no carpets, a pet and some objects on the floor. Space can be found for the station. Strong preference for an automatic dust-emptying, mop-washing and mop-drying dock. Roborock is a preference, not a requirement.

Budget is undecided. Recommendations are provisional because independent obstacle-avoidance performance, pet-waste detection and Turkish service coverage have not been established. With a pet and clutter, these checks matter more than raw suction. Never assume a robot can safely avoid pet accidents.

For this home prioritize conservative water control for laminate, obstacle avoidance, pet-hair maintenance, clean mop pads, edge coverage, automatic dock care and service support. Check the flooring manufacturer permits damp robotic mopping; start with low water and do not allow pooling. Automatic stations still require clean-water refills, dirty-water disposal and occasional manual cleaning. No plumbed-in station is confirmed in this shortlist. Extra suction, enormous batteries, carpet handling and fancy threshold climbing deserve little premium. This is a feature-based comparison, not a controlled cleaning-performance test.

The recommendation for your home

Leading candidate: Ecovacs T80S OMNI — conditional on Turkish model equivalence and warranty.

The strongest retrieved combination of measured obstacle avoidance, low hair tangling and mopping with little residual water makes it the most relevant candidate for your laminate floor, pet and some clutter. This replaces the earlier specs-led Xiaomi recommendation and premature Dreame L40 recommendation.

Buying decision: wait or negotiate, not rush. The retrieved ₺32,374.08 listing is above the dated ₺26,975 observation from 8 November. That earlier price is a reference, not a promise of its return. Confirm that Turkish stock matches the tested T80S, not T80.

  • Runner-up: original Dreame L40 Ultra, preferably with the exact TriCut brush verified. Good obstacle and stain-cleaning evidence, but standard-brush hair problems and conflicting bare-floor results stop it being the clear winner.
  • If you prefer Roborock: Qrevo L Pro is a maintenance-cleaning option, not a demonstrated clutter specialist. Actual reviews report cable tangles and shoes being pushed. Edge 5V1 has too little exact-model testing to rank confidently.
  • Xiaomi X20 Max is no longer my default value pick for you. Excess water and hair tangling in independent tests are particularly relevant to laminate and pets.
  • Budget remains open. These are fit-based recommendations, not an instruction to spend ₺30–40k. Below that range, prioritize verified local service and automatic pad care rather than an uncertain flagship import.

No robot here is proven safe around pet accidents or loose cables. Pick those up before scheduled runs. Automatic docks still need water handling and periodic cleaning.

Before paying

  • Confirm exact model suffix, colour, included station and Turkish SKU; similar names are not interchangeable.
  • Ask who provides warranty/service in Turkey. “Türkiye garantili”, marketplace branding and “official store” titles alone are not independent authorization checks.
  • Compare like-for-like checkout prices: membership, coupons, card discounts, shipping, invoice issuer and return terms.
  • Measure installed station depth plus approach space, electrical outlet position and overhead room to lift tanks. Body dimensions alone are insufficient.
  • For wood/laminate, check the floor manufacturer's wet-cleaning guidance and start with conservative water settings. A hot dock washes the mop: it does not mean the robot washes the floor with 55–75°C water.
  • Expect to refill clean water, empty/rinse dirty water, remove hair and periodically clean trays/pads. Automatic washing does not make any robot maintenance-free.
  • Check local prices/availability for replacement pads or roller, filters, dust bags and approved cleaning solution.

What would change the recommendation?

  • Tight budget: Xiaomi is the starting point; broaden to simpler docks only if manual pad washing is acceptable.
  • Frequent sticky spills: investigate T80S roller design, with independent cleaning evidence and local SKU confirmation before a premium purchase.
  • Very tight dock space: use installed dimensions and tank access, not “Mini” branding.
  • Lots of hair/pets: anti-tangle design and replacement brush costs matter more.
  • Floor clutter: obstacle avoidance performance becomes more important than peak suction.
